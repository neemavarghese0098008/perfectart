More About Perfect Art Contracting Company

Our Story

Perfect Art Contracting Company was founded in Riyadh in 2013. Over more than a decade, we have grown into one of Saudi Arabia's leading contractors, delivering residential and commercial projects across the Kingdom, the UAE, and Bahrain. We are trusted by names including GymNation, Marriott, Tim Hortons, Adidas, Nando's, Fitness First, Steve Madden, Brands for Less, and Home Box, while still treating every villa and every home project with the same attention as our largest commercial contracts.
